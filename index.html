how it looks.

It’s how it works, how it feels, and how naturally people find their way through it.

I’m a UI/UX designer focused on user research, interface design and digital product experiences. I enjoy getting close to the problem, understanding what people need, and shaping that into interfaces that feel simple and purposeful.

I work mainly in Figma, from early flows and wireframes to polished UI, interactive prototypes and design systems. I also collaborate with developers and creative teams to help good ideas make it into the real world.
